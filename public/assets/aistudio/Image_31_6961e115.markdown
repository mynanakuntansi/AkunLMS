## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-trust, disciplined, and technologically adept visual environment for vocational accounting education (SMK Akuntansi dan Keuangan Lembaga). It merges modern software utility with the rigorous precision of institutional financial tooling. 

The aesthetic model is **Corporate Modern with Dense Data-Centric Utility**. It eschews decorative whimsy in favor of structural integrity, tabular clarity, high-contrast legibility, and crisp informational hierarchies. Interfaces must project stability, procedural rigor, and clarity, transforming complex ledger sheets, journals, and reconciliation tasks into intuitive, calm, and actionable digital learning environments.

## Colors

The color system operates on an institutional hierarchy rooted in financial balance:

- **Primary (`#0F2942` Deep Navy / `#1E3A8A` Midnight Accent):** Symbolizes fiduciary responsibility, discipline, and institutional authority. Used for primary navigation shells, high-level headers, active structural frames, and primary call-to-actions.
- **Secondary (`#059669` / `#10B981` Emerald Ledger):** Symbolizes accuracy, balanced accounts, verified entries, and successful module completion. Used selectively for credit validations, balanced badges, and confirmed actions.
- **Tertiary (`#0284C7` Cyan/Cerulean):** Used for focal markers, interactive focus rings, active step indicators, hyperlinks, and simulated journal cursor indicators.
- **Surface & Canvas (`#F8FAFC` Slate/Ice White with `#FFFFFF` Cards):** Ensures zero eye strain across extended double-entry journal auditing and ledger reviews.
- **Semantic Signals:**
  - *Balanced / Selesai:* Emerald (`#059669` text on `#ECFDF5` background).
  - *Perlu Diperiksa (Pending/Review):* Amber (`#D97706` text on `#FFFBEB` background).
  - *Terlambat / Unbalanced (Selisih):* Crimson (`#DC2626` text on `#FEF2F2` background).

## Typography

Typography prioritizes functional scannability:
- **Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Provide modern, balanced authority with geometric clarity for section titles, competency modules, and performance overviews.
- **Body & Labels (Inter):** Highly legible across variable screen densities. All numerical calculations, accounts, debits, credits, and ledger cells must activate CSS `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums lining-nums` to guarantee vertical decimal alignment across rows.
- **Data & Formula Overrides (JetBrains Mono):** Reserved for transaction IDs, voucher codes, general ledger chart-of-account numbers (Kode Akun), and real-time formula bars.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout employs a responsive, fluid 12-column grid system with fixed gutters tailored to structured enterprise tooling:
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12 columns, 24px gutters, 32px canvas margin. Max content container: 1600px for multi-column reconciliation workbenches.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8 columns, 16px gutters, 24px canvas margin.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 16px gutters, 16px canvas margin.

A strict 4px/8px incremental rhythm is maintained. Spreadsheets and ledger views use a high-density vertical cadence (cell padding of 6px to 8px) to maximize visible balance sheet information above the fold.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy shadows, using a combination of **low-contrast structural outlines** and **subtle surface stacking**:

- **Level 0 (Canvas):** `#F8FAFC` base application background.
- **Level 1 (Cards, Ledger Sheets):** `#FFFFFF` solid background with a 1px border of `#E2E8F0`. No shadow is needed in rest state.
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns, Floating Palettes, Action Menus):** `#FFFFFF` with a 1px border of `#CBD5E1` and an ambient shadow: `0px 4px 16px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Dialogs, Trial Balance Inspection Drawers):** `0px 12px 32px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.16)`.
- **Active Grid Cells / Selection Focus:** Zero blur, 2px solid `#0284C7` ring with a 1px white internal offset, replicating high-productivity accounting spreadsheet feedback.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a **Soft (`1`)** roundedness profile (4px base radius for form controls, 6px-8px for cards and containers).

- **Grid Cells and Data Inputs:** 2px to 4px corners, reinforcing structural discipline.
- **Cards and Module Panels:** 8px (`rounded-lg`), delivering crisp containment without distracting visual softness.
- **Status Badges & Pill Tags:** Full pill radius (`rounded-full`) exclusively used for operational statuses to distinguish metadata from editable data cells.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#0F2942`, text `#FFFFFF`, hover `#1E3A8A`, active `#0B1E30`. Padding: 8px 16px; border-radius: 4px.
- **Secondary:** Surface `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#CBD5E1`, text `#0F2942`, hover `#F1F5F9`.
- **Success/Audit:** Background `#059669`, text `#FFFFFF`, hover `#047857`. Used for actions like "Tutup Buku" or "Validasi Jurnal".

### Data Tables & Interactive Ledger Grid
- **Header:** Background `#F1F5F9`, border-bottom 2px solid `#CBD5E1`, uppercase text in `label-sm` (`#475569`).
- **Cells:** Border 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, padding 6px 12px.
- **Alignment:** Alphanumeric/Kode Akun/Keterangan are left-aligned; Reference (Ref) is centered; Debit and Credit values are strictly right-aligned with monospace tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).
- **Total/Balance Row:** Background `#F8FAFC`, top border 1px solid `#0F2942`, bottom border double 3px solid `#0F2942`, bold weights.

### Status Badges
- Compact padding: 2px 8px, font-size 11px, weight 600, border radius `9999px`.
- **Seimbang / Selesai:** Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#065F46`, border 1px solid `#A7F3D0`.
- **Perlu Diperiksa:** Background `#FFFBEB`, text `#92400E`, border 1px solid `#FDE68A`.
- **Terlambat / Selisih:** Background `#FEF2F2`, text `#991B1B`, border 1px solid `#FECACA`.

### Input Fields & Transaction Rows
- Standard inputs use 1px border `#CBD5E1`, background `#FFFFFF`, text `body-md`. Focus transitions to a 2px outline in `#0284C7`. Error validation displays a red inline label with `#DC2626`.

### Cards & Metric Displays
- **Metric Cards (KPIs):** Clean container with a 1px border `#E2E8F0`, top accent bar (3px) in Primary Navy or Emerald Green, displaying title, large tabular metric, and micro-trend indicator.
